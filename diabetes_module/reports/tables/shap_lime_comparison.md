### Concordance Synthesis
1. **High Agreement on Primary Clinical Drivers**: Both SHAP and LIME consistently rank **Age**, **BMI**, and **Told High Blood Pressure** as the dominant drivers across all risk strata.
2. **Directional Consistency**: For low-risk patients, both methods attribute strong protective credit to younger age and normal BMI. For high-risk patients, both assign substantial positive attribution to elevated BMI (>30 kg/m2) and hypertensive status.
3. **Methodological Differences**: SHAP benefits from global consistency and additivity ($f(x) = E[f(x)] + \sum \phi_i$), guaranteeing exact baseline-to-output convergence, whereas LIME relies on perturbed sampling around the instance which yields slight coefficient variance across random seeds.
